\section{ESG Indicators as Actually Constructed}
\label{app:indicators}

Table~\ref{tab:indicators} lists every ESG indicator that carries
cross-sectional variation in the mid-cap universe, together with the source
that actually fills it. ``Sector pct.'' means the within-sector percentile of
the stated ratio, clipped to $[2,98]$. When a source is missing, a cell falls
through to the next tier of Section~\ref{sec:esg}. The other
\NIndUnused declared indicators have no source for any mid-cap
firm and are excluded from scoring. They are: Scope~2 and~3 emissions,
emissions intensity, energy efficiency, water intensity, environmental fines,
R\&D intensity (E innovation), employee turnover, women in management, pay
gap, injury rate, CEO pay ratio and the anti-corruption policy flag.

\section{Audit Log}
\label{app:audit}

All corrections below were made before any post-snapshot price was
downloaded.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{ESG direction.} \texttt{scope1\_emissions} and
  \texttt{esg\_risk\_rating} were removed from the lower-is-better set,
  because both are stored as higher-is-better scores.
\item \emph{Imputation noise.} Multiplicative $\mathcal{N}(1,0.04)$ noise in
  sector and global median imputation was removed.
\item \emph{Registry data.} Registry memberships are encoded as 0/1 and no
  longer overwrite continuous emissions and renewable-share columns. A
  mapping of a 40--70 score onto the binary anti-corruption flag, which made
  that flag constant, was removed.
\item \emph{Source precedence.} ISS governance scores now take precedence
  over SEC financial items mapped onto governance columns. A syntax error in
  this step had prevented the download script from running.
\item \emph{Monotonicity correction.} The isotonic fit to the validation
  proxy is disabled by default (\texttt{monotonicity\_correction: false}).
\item \emph{Preference transforms.} The undocumented bottom-30\% quality gate
  and the power exponent of 1.5 are disabled by default.
\item \emph{Snapshot consistency.} FX is taken at the snapshot rate.
  \texttt{market\_share} is recomputed after currency conversion. Firms that
  did not trade on the snapshot date are removed.
\item \emph{Portfolio size.} $N^\star=\lceil 0.07N\rceil$, bounded to
  $[10,50]$, replaces a rounding rule with a special case for $N=276$.
\item \emph{Rank uncertainty.} The score-perturbing ``Bayesian bootstrap'' was
  replaced by the procedure of Section~\ref{sec:rank_design}.
\item \emph{Manuscript.} Several results in an earlier draft could not be
  reproduced from the code and were removed. These were walk-forward ICs,
  Fama--MacBeth estimates, an ablation table, a claimed sign flip of the
  value factor, and ``100\% of periods'' out-performance of a momentum proxy.
  Two further claims were withdrawn:
  \begin{itemize}
  \item a large-cap ``generalisation'' statistic ($\rho\approx1$), which
    measured only the effect of adding 45 firms to the percentile reference
    set;
  \item a Kendall's $W$ that measured agreement between investor profiles,
    not generalisation.
  \end{itemize}
  A residualisation of E on the financial score, described in the draft, was
  never executed by the pipeline and is not claimed here.
\end{enumerate}
