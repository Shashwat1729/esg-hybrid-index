\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a transparent nine-factor ESG--financial index for \NMid US and
Indian mid-caps and audited it end to end. Its E and S pillars turn out to
be mostly within-sector financial quality, with other factors explaining
\RSqE and \RSqS of their variance, and only the governance pillar carries
largely independent information. The in-sample evidence that made the index
look strong came from validation targets that were not independent of the
index. Out of sample, the composite does not predict returns
(IC $=\HOneEst$), but higher ESG predicts lower future volatility beyond
trailing volatility (partial $\rho=\HTwoEst$, Holm $p=\HTwoHolm$) and beyond
\NControls known risk characteristics and sector effects. That risk signal
comes from the proxy-built S pillar rather than from measured cells
(\PartMeas), adds \CVDeltaESG to an out-of-fold volatility forecast, and
falls to \RecFirst given recent realised volatility. In a rising market the
tilt was costly, with an ESG-only top-\TopN screen returning \ExESGFull~pp
relative to the universe.

The contribution is as much methodological as empirical. Provenance per
cell, redundancy per pillar, a dated construction and an out-of-sample test
with fixed hypotheses are cheap to implement, and together they separate a
transparent composite from a valid one. The natural next step is the
pre-registered window (\PreregAnchor to \PreregEnd), which will confirm or
refute the risk result against recent-volatility controls. Beyond it, the
same snapshot--hold-out cycle can be repeated each quarter to accumulate
power, and replacing the financial proxies with measured E and S data, such
as EPA TRI records, the Business Responsibility and Sustainability Reports
(BRSR) of Indian firms and text-mined sustainability reports
\cite{luccioni_2020}, would show whether genuine environmental and social
measurement shares the risk signal.

\textbf{Reproducibility.} The code, configuration, cached raw data, frozen
pre-audit outputs and the registration package are in the project
repository\RepoNote. A single command rebuilds every table, figure and
number in this paper from the \SnapshotDate snapshot and the cached
post-snapshot prices, and the repository documents each step, including the
evaluation of the registered window once it has closed.
