\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We built a transparent nine-factor ESG--financial index for \NMid US and
Indian mid-caps and audited it end to end. The audit answers four questions.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{What does public-data ESG measure?} Its E and S pillars are
  mostly financial quality measured within sector (other factors explain
  \RSqE and \RSqS of their variance). Only the governance pillar carries
  largely independent information.
\item \emph{Is common in-sample evidence informative?} No. Trailing-return
  ``ICs'', a contemporaneous ``forward'' proxy, a correction fitted to that
  proxy and a misapplied bootstrap together made a composite with no
  demonstrable predictive power look strong.
\item \emph{What happens out of sample?} The composite does not predict
  returns (IC $=\HOneEst$), and its top-\TopN portfolio does not beat the
  universe. Higher ESG does predict lower future volatility beyond trailing
  volatility (partial $\rho=\HTwoEst$, Holm $p=\HTwoHolm$). In a rising
  market this risk tilt was costly: an ESG-only top-\TopN screen had an excess
  return of \ExESGFull~pp.
\item \emph{Is that risk signal ESG?} Only in name. It survives nine known risk
  characteristics and sector effects (\HFourEst), but it comes from
  financial-proxy cells (measured cells: \PartMeas), adds \CVDeltaESG to an
  out-of-fold volatility forecast, and falls to \RecFirst given recent
  realised volatility.
\end{enumerate}

The contribution is as much methodological as empirical. Transparency is
necessary but not sufficient for validity. Provenance per cell, redundancy
per pillar, a dated construction and an out-of-sample test with fixed
hypotheses are cheap to implement and should accompany any ESG index built
from public data.

\textbf{Future work.} Future work should:
\begin{itemize}
\item evaluate the pre-registered window (\PreregAnchor to \PreregEnd), which
  will confirm or refute H2 and H4 against recent-volatility controls;
\item repeat the snapshot--hold-out cycle quarterly, to accumulate power;
\item replace financial proxies with measured E and S data (EPA TRI, BRSR
  filings for Indian firms, and text-mined sustainability reports
  \cite{luccioni_2020});
\item test whether the ESG--volatility association persists across market
  regimes, and whether measured E and S data share it.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Reproducibility.} The code, configuration, cached raw data and
frozen pre-audit outputs are in the project repository. Running
\texttt{python scripts/run\_all.py --skip-download} rebuilds every table,
figure and number in this paper from the \SnapshotDate snapshot and the cached
post-snapshot prices; \texttt{--refresh} re-downloads them.
